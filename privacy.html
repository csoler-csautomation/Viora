Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Viora is an accessibility-focused application currently in development. This policy describes the intended handling of information in Viora. It will be updated before public release to reflect the final production features and service providers.

Information Viora may process

Depending on the features a user chooses, Viora may process photos or camera images, questions submitted to the assistant, saved clothing or product information, accessibility preferences, and app settings.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo access is used only when the user chooses features that require visual input. Production behavior, retention periods, and any cloud processing will be disclosed in this policy before release.

AI features

Some Viora features are planned to use third-party artificial intelligence services to analyze user-provided content and generate responses. The final production policy will identify applicable providers and describe what data is sent.

Shopping and affiliate links

Viora may display links to third-party retailers. Some links may be affiliate links, which may allow Viora to earn a commission from qualifying purchases. Retailers operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Children

Viora is not currently designed as a service directed to children.

Security and retention

Viora will use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information it processes. Final retention practices will be documented before public release.

Your choices

Users will be able to control camera/photo permissions through iOS and manage applicable saved content within the app as those features are finalized.

Changes

This policy may change as Viora develops. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact information published on the Viora Support page.
